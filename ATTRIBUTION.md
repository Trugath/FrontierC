# Original Frontier: Elite II Assets

Original artwork extracted from *Frontier: Elite II* (1993).

- **Original bitmap artwork:** David Braben and Paul Mitchell
- **Original 3D models and designs:** David Braben, Jonathan Griffiths, Peter J. M. Irvin and Aggy Finn
- **Original game:** David Braben
- **Original copyright:** Frontier Developments Ltd. (subject to the applicable original asset rights)

This repository does not contain those assets, or the game's descriptions,
dialogue, title card, interface and notice lines, AdLib soundtrack, galaxy
density grid, and setup messages. `frontier-data unpack` reads them from your
own copy of the game and writes indexed PNGs, model sources, a bitmap font,
palettes, MIDI, those text files, the soundtrack sources, and the density grid
on your machine.
Extraction and format conversion: Elliot Stirling.
